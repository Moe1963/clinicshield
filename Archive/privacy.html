Section 1

About ClinicShield

ClinicShield is a compliance management dashboard operated by Mojo Control Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 33 563 327 309), trading as ClinicShield ("we", "us", "our"). Our registered business address is Point Cook, Victoria, Australia.

ClinicShield is designed to help Australian medical practice managers track staff credentials, AHPRA registrations, sterilisation validation records, and Medicare readiness in a single dashboard.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Section 2

What Data We Collect

When you use ClinicShield, we collect the following information:

Practice Information

  • Practice name, type, and state
  • Licence key used to access the dashboard

Staff Information

  • Staff member names and roles
  • AHPRA registration numbers and expiry dates
  • CPR and anaphylaxis training expiry dates

Compliance Records

  • Sterilisation validation dates (AS 5369)
  • Medicare readiness checklist responses

Waitlist Information

  • Email addresses submitted via the waitlist form on our website

We do not collect: patient records, clinical notes, financial data, tax file numbers, Medicare billing data, or any information directly identifying patients.

Section 3

How We Use Your Data

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate the ClinicShield compliance dashboard
  • To display Red/Amber/Green compliance status for your practice
  • To save and retrieve your compliance records between sessions
  • To send you product updates and beta access information (waitlist emails only)
  • To improve the product based on usage patterns

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the ClinicShield service.

Section 4

Data Storage & Security

Your compliance data is stored securely using Upstash Redis, a cloud database service hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest.

Important: During the current beta period, data is stored on AWS Singapore region servers. We are working to migrate to AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2) to ensure all data remains within Australia. We will notify beta users when this migration is complete.

Access to your dashboard data is protected by your unique licence key. We strongly recommend keeping your licence key confidential and not sharing it with unauthorised parties.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 5

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share data with the following service providers solely to operate ClinicShield:

  • Upstash — database storage (data processing only, not for their own purposes)
  • Vercel — application hosting and delivery
  • Formspree — waitlist email collection

We may disclose information if required by Australian law, court order, or regulatory authority.

Section 6

Data Retention

We retain your compliance data for as long as your ClinicShield licence is active. If you request deletion of your data, we will permanently remove all records associated with your licence key within 30 days.

Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request removal.

Section 7

Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about your practice
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if you believe your privacy rights have been breached

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at moe@mojocontrolsolutions.com.au.

Section 8

Cookies
